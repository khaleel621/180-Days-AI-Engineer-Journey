## DSA: Counting Occurrences

Implemented `count_occurrences(numbers, target)` in `02-data-structures-algorithms/arrays/day05-count-occurrences.py`:

- Counter initialized to zero, one `for` loop over the list, increment when an element equals the target, return the final count.
- No `.count()` or `collections.Counter`.
- Handles empty lists and targets that do not occur (returns 0).
- Example: `[2, 5, 2, 8, 2, 10, 5]` with target `2` → `2 appears 3 times`.
- Complexity: O(n) time, O(1) extra space.
