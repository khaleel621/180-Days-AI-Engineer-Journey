### DSA: Evaluate Reverse Polish Notation (Stack)

Implemented `eval_rpn(tokens)` in
`02-data-structures-algorithms/stacks/day14-evaluate-reverse-polish-notation.py`:

**Concepts learned:**
- Reverse Polish Notation (postfix): operators follow their operands.
- Use a stack (Python list with `append`/`pop`).
- Push numbers onto the stack; on an operator, pop the right operand first,
  then the left operand, compute, and push the result.
- Operand order matters for `-` and `/`.
- Division truncates toward zero via `int(a / b)`.
- Negative number tokens like "-7" are converted with `int()`.
- `eval()` is NOT used.

**Complexity:** Time O(n); Space O(n).

**Tests:** `test_day14_evaluate_reverse_polish_notation.py` - all 11 tests
passed, covering all six required cases plus multiplication, addition, negative
intermediate results, division truncation toward zero, and a single operand.
